Objective: Design a competition-ready fully 3D-printed excavation rover for sandy terrain material collection and deposition within a 30×30×30 cm envelope — leading all engineering subteams from concept through competition.

Scope: As Rover Systems Lead, directed five concurrent subteams: drivetrain, payload, chassis, electronics, and system integration testing. Authored a 160-page engineering report documenting simulation, electronics, DFAM/DFMA, and testing.

Validation: Sand-bed testing across multiple configurations; modal analysis on gimbal; FEA across drivetrain and structural components.

50-member team 5 subteams led 30×30×30 cm 160-page report Sand-validated

Track System: Custom pin-jointed modular track architecture with sprocket-driven traction and integrated guide features to maintain belt alignment under abrasive lateral loading. Individual link replacement without full disassembly.

Suspension: Spring-based system validated through sandbox testing — maintains traction on uneven terrain while keeping the chassis level for payload operations.

Validation: Worst-case static load at sprocket contact interfaces. Bench testing confirmed stable engagement and minimal derailment across sand-bed passes.

Stable tracked motion Spring suspension Modular / field-repairable Sand-optimized geometry
